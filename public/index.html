Listening cursor

Listen to one spot

Each move plays one 10 degree square of the current map and says its temperature. The globe turns to show it. You can also tap the globe.

Or jump to a place

Each button plays the 10 degree square of sea at that place, in the current map, and turns the globe there. Cities are coastal: you hear the sea beside them, not the air.
